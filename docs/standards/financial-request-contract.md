# Financial request contract standard

## Scope and relationship to fiduciary rules

This standard governs the intake, normalization, and verification of quantitative financial models, portfolio allocations, risk stress tests, and SEC EDGAR analysis within `financial-advisement-router`.

Every report, allocation, valuation, and simulation generated under this standard is a non-discretionary research product. It is not individual investment advice, not a personal recommendation, and not an offer to buy or sell securities.

## Normalized request contract

All operations MUST normalize user instructions into a machine-verifiable record conforming to [`financial-request-v1.schema.json`](./financial-request-v1.schema.json) before calculation:

```text
Request/version: [id; version; operation: dcf_valuation | portfolio_optimization | var_risk_simulation | lbo_modeling | edgar_xbrl_extract | comps_benchmark | rebalancing_drift | tax_loss_harvest]
Portfolio profile: [portfolio id; currency: ISO 4217; risk profile; horizon; tax status]
Model parameters: [decimal precision places: 2 to 28; seeded PRNG: true; seed integer; benchmark index]
Intent: [primary objective; analytical scope; target return; max drawdown limit]
Representations: [canonical representations; primary vs supporting; delivery format]
Constraints: [allocation bounds, leverage limits, sector caps, tracking error limits] (hard | soft)
References: [financial statements, 10-K/10-Q CIKs, macro factor series, index benchmarks]
Compliance & disclosures: [advisory stamp required: true; FINRA 2214 legend required: true; no live order tickets: true; synthetic issuers only: true]
Audience & delivery: [recipient role; format: markdown | json | html | csv | xlsx; trading authorization granted: false]
Provenance: [model tier; timestamp UTC]
Open questions: [macro assumptions, cash drag rules, dividend reinvestment policy]
Review & handoff: [owner: authoring operator; reviewer: fiduciary-compliance-auditor; acceptance tests; advisory stamp verified]
```

## Non-negotiable numerical standards

1. **Strict IEEE 754 Decimal Arithmetic**:
   - Money, NAV, asset shares, and returns MUST be processed using Python's `decimal.Decimal`.
   - Floating-point representations (`float`, `double`) are strictly prohibited in ledgers and balances.
   - Any financial deliverable containing raw float arithmetic will fail validation.

2. **Seeded Reproducibility**:
   - Any Monte Carlo or stochastic risk simulation MUST require an explicit integer PRNG seed so results are 100% deterministic and reproducible.

## Mandatory non-discretionary advisory legend

Every deliverable generated by `financial-advisement-router` MUST display the standard advisory notice:

```text
NON-DISCRETIONARY FINANCIAL RESEARCH PRODUCT - FOR ANALYTICAL PURPOSES ONLY - NOT INDIVIDUAL INVESTMENT ADVICE - NOT A RECOMMENDATION TO BUY OR SELL ANY SECURITY OR ADOPT ANY INVESTMENT STRATEGY
```

For Monte Carlo or outcome likelihood tools, the FINRA Rule 2214(c)(4) legend MUST also be included:
```text
IMPORTANT: Projections or other information generated by this analytical tool regarding the likelihood of various investment outcomes are hypothetical in nature, do not reflect actual investment results, and are not guarantees of future results.
```
